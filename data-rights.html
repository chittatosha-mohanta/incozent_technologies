Exercise Your Data Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you are entitled to request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, or to withdraw previously given consent.

Submit a Data Rights Request

Request Received

Your data rights request has been logged. Our Grievance Officer will review your request, verify identity, and respond within the statutory timeframe (acknowledgment within 48 hours; resolution within 30 days).

How Rights Requests Work

Incozent complies strictly with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Requests are subject to identity verification to prevent unauthorized disclosure or malicious deletion.

  • Identity Check: We cross-reference the email and details on file.
  • No Processing Fee: Exercising your statutory rights is free of charge.
  • Statutory Timelines: Formal resolution delivered within 30 days.
