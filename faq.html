FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about how we collect, handle and deliver real-world data.

General
What does Incozent actually do?

We design and run custom real-world data collection programs for AI companies — covering participant recruitment, collection, curation, annotation, quality control, privacy review and delivery.

Do you have ready-made datasets available for purchase?

No. We build collection programs around each customer's specification rather than selling from a fixed inventory. Our data catalog page shows illustrative collection capabilities, not ready-to-buy datasets.

What types of data can you collect?

Facial and biometric data, computer vision data, images, video, egocentric/first-person data, robotics and physical AI data, autonomous driving data, human activity data, and custom categories designed for your specific project.

Privacy & Consent
How is participant consent handled?

Every participant is informed of the purpose, usage and handling of their data and provides consent before any collection takes place. Consent processes are defined per project. See our Privacy & Consent page for details.

Can I upload biometric data through this website?

No. This website does not accept public biometric uploads. Custom collection is planned and executed on a per-project basis with defined consent procedures.

How long is data retained?

Retention periods and deletion procedures are defined per project and communicated to participants as part of the consent process.

Quality & Process
What quality guarantees do you offer?

Quality criteria — including accuracy thresholds and acceptance standards — are defined per project. We validate against your defined criteria at every pipeline stage rather than promising a fixed universal number.

Do I need to start with a pilot?

It's strongly recommended for larger projects. A pilot lets you validate methodology, quality and delivery format at small scale before committing to full collection.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on data type, volume, geography and complexity. We provide a project-specific timeline after reviewing your requirements.

Working With Us
How do I start a project?

Submit a request through our Request a Dataset form, or contact us directly. We'll follow up to scope your requirements and recommend a pilot or full project approach.

Can I become a data collection participant?

Yes — you can register your interest through our Join the Participant Network page. This records interest only and does not collect biometric samples or guarantee participation.
