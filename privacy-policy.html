Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 2026. This Privacy Notice is issued in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and outlines how Incozent Technologies processes and protects personal data.

Data Fiduciary Notice: Incozent Technologies acts as a Data Fiduciary regarding contact and inquiry submissions. We process personal data solely based on free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent.

1. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We collect only minimal personal data through our website forms:

  • Identity & Professional Contact Details: Full Name, Business Email Address, Phone Number, Job Title, and Company/Organization Name.
  • Inquiry & Project Specifications: Project description, technical dataset requirements, geographic targets, and delivery timelines.
  • Participant Network Registrations: Name, Email, Country, City, Age Range, Device Type, Languages, and non-sensitive interest categories.
  • Technical & Consent Telemetry: Timestamp of submission, consent checkbox records, and standard server communication logs.
Strict Non-Collection of Biometrics via Website: Incozent does NOT collect selfies, facial scans, biometric samples, voice recordings, or government identity cards via this public website. All custom AI biometric collections are executed under standalone, project-specific, in-person/instrumented protocols with dedicated informed consent agreements.

2. Lawful Purposes for Processing

In accordance with Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, your personal data is collected and processed strictly for the following specified purposes:

  • To respond to, communicate regarding, and resolve commercial inquiries submitted by you.
  • To evaluate, design, scope, and plan custom AI dataset collection programs and feasibility pilots.
  • To notify registered participants regarding eligible data collection opportunities matching their expressed profile.
  • To manage statutory compliance, audit verification, and defend against security incidents.

3. Data Retention Period

Personal data collected via website inquiries is retained for a period of 180 days from the date of submission, or until the purpose of processing has been fulfilled, unless a longer retention period is required by Indian law or under an executed commercial contract. Once the retention period lapses or upon your verified request for erasure, data is safely purged from our systems.

4. Third-Party Processors & Data Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize trusted service providers (Data Processors) bound by stringent data protection obligations:

  • Google Workspace & Google Apps Script: Automated workflow routing and encrypted spreadsheet storage (Google Cloud infrastructure with AES-256 at-rest encryption and TLS in transit).
  • Vercel Inc.: Secure website hosting, edge delivery, and transport security.

5. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Sections 11 to 14 of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of personal data being processed and identities of Data Processors with whom it has been shared.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: You may request updating or correction of inaccurate, misleading, or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the unconditional right to withdraw your consent at any time without negative consequences.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to an accessible mechanism for resolving any grievances with Incozent.
  • Right to Nominate: You may designate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, may exercise your data rights.

To exercise any of these rights, you may submit a request through our online Data Rights Portal or email our Grievance Officer directly.
