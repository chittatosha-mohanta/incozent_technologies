Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy describes how we handle information collected through this website and through project-based data collection programs.

1. Overview

This policy covers (a) information submitted through this website's forms (contact, dataset request, pilot request, participant registration) and (b) data collected as part of project-based collection programs, which is additionally governed by project-specific consent agreements.

2. Information We Collect via This Website

Contact details, company information, project requirements and, for participant registration, non-sensitive interest and eligibility information. We do not collect biometric samples, selfies, facial videos or identity documents through this website.

3. How We Use Information

To respond to business inquiries, scope projects, evaluate pilot requests, and contact registered participants about relevant future opportunities.

4. Project-Specific Data

Any data collected for a specific customer project — including facial, biometric, image, video or activity data — is governed by a project-specific consent process, described in our Privacy & Consent page, and is not covered by website form submissions.

5. Data Retention

Website form submissions are retained as needed to respond to your inquiry. Project data retention periods are defined per project.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted by contacting us.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page.
