About

Building Real-World Data Infrastructure for AI

We are building an India-first real-world data infrastructure company for AI teams that require custom, consented and quality-controlled datasets.

Mission

To help AI companies get the real-world data their models need — collected responsibly, curated carefully and delivered reliably.

Vision

A world where AI teams can access diverse, real-world, consented data as easily as they access compute — without compromising on privacy or quality.

What We Are Building

A structured operating model for real-world data collection — spanning recruitment, collection, curation, annotation, quality control and privacy-aware delivery — built around each customer's specification.

Why Real-World Data Matters

Public and synthetic datasets often fail to reflect a model's actual target users, devices, geography or environment. Real-world, purpose-collected data closes that gap.

Why India

India offers an unusually broad range of demographic, cultural, environmental, urban, rural, mobility and device conditions — a strong foundation for building a collection operating model, with future expansion in mind.

Data Philosophy

Data should be designed around the model, not the other way around. Every project starts with your requirement, not a pre-existing inventory.

Quality Philosophy

Quality criteria are defined per project and validated at every pipeline stage — not promised as a fixed, universal number.

Privacy Philosophy

Human and biometric data collection requires a defined purpose and informed consent before it begins — not as an afterthought.
